# Complete canon reading — September 2026

Governing standard: preserve the wonder; strive for simplicity, beauty, and humanity; keep a point of view willing to be wrong.

This pass follows the completed style guide. It reads the eleven current canonical essays and the still-readable, superseded *Irreducible* in full, including appendices, tables, captions, components, and reference annotations. It is a complete editorial reading, not a fresh independent replication of every model or source.

## Reading and decisions

Entries are added after each complete reading and revision. Strong passages are protected before changes are made. The original reading order, historical status, publication metadata, equations, and source links remain in place unless a specific correction is recorded.
