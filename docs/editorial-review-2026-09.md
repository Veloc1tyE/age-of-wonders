# Canon editorial review — September 2026

## Finding

The soul of Age of Wonders is the encounter between physical abundance and a particular human life. Its best prose makes a vast possibility intelligible through a small, exact thing: a hand reaching for a camera, aerogel on a petal, a sewing machine waiting for power, a ship carrying its own harbour.

The voice is most persuasive when it trusts that encounter. It also needs room around it: the welcome of a manifesto, a metaphor that keeps unfolding, a future the reader can imagine entering. The governing standard, refined through Billy's response to the first pass, is: **Preserve the wonder. Strive for simplicity, beauty, and humanity.**

The new [style guide](../STYLE_GUIDE.md) develops this judgement through close readings from the entire collection. It replaces the former prose section of [CLAUDE.md](../CLAUDE.md); the site's operational instructions remain there. The essay template now points writers towards the guide and removes its decorative equation, generic conclusion, and instruction to use bold for emphasis.

## Scope

Read all twelve non-template essay bodies, including headings, descriptions, components, captions, share copy, and endings. Compared appendix claim registers, limitations, and selected derivations with their claims in the body. This was an editorial review with targeted factual checks, not a fresh technical validation of every calculation or a source-by-source audit of every reference.

The current site sequence has eleven essays. *Symbolic Superintelligence* occupies the ninth position. *Irreducible* remains available at its historical route with `supersededBy: symbolic-superintelligence`. That relationship, all essay publication flags and dates, frontmatter titles and descriptions, and canonical ordering were preserved.

No deployment, commit, distribution, or external publication was performed. The service-worker cache version was advanced so these content revisions can reach returning readers when a deployment is authorised.

## What the guide changes

The earlier guide contained valuable observations but increasingly treated their surface forms as requirements. It required all five “magic patterns”, a short compression drop, and progressively shorter endings. It also instructed writers to purge hedges and treat some future thresholds as inevitable. Those rules could produce confident prose without the evidence needed to sustain it.

The revised guide keeps the useful devices and removes the quotas. It teaches the function behind a device: physical contact, an intelligible mechanism, a consequential distinction, room to breathe, or an earned ending. It makes a keep list part of revision, so editing begins by identifying what should survive.

The guide also separates kinds of authority. Observation, calculation, inference, proposal, prediction, speculation, and belief can all belong in the canon. They cannot borrow one another's certainty. The ambition survives that distinction; the reader can see where the work remains.

The decisive test is whether a passage becomes easier to understand without becoming less alive. The transmission opening is longer after revision and easier to read. The starship's skim is shorter and more physical. The final line of *Love is the Foundation* remains longer than the old limit because its verbs need that room.

## The second pass: preserve the wonder

Billy identified the first pass's main error: CAOW's more explicit opening was less beautiful. A manifesto needs some of its vague, human language. It lets the reader enter a possibility before the writer specifies its mechanisms.

The second pass compared the revisions with the original for welcome, music, humanity, and room to imagine. It restored CAOW's first two paragraphs; “Night is becoming a manufacturing problem”; the thread of light carrying data home; the extended meditation beginning “The scale will not answer”; and the gathering of earlier essays at the close of *Love* and *Singularity*. Those repetitions carry a return, not merely a summary.

Billy also singled out “This is a physical bound, not a machine we know how to build.” That sentence had told the reader where to stop imagining. It is gone. The black hole's lifetime remains visible in the callout and calculations. Elsewhere, drag takes its share, a vehicle's structure must fit its mass allowance, and a message sent before a software rollback still has its recipient. The things themselves show the constraints.

The guide now begins with Billy's governing standard. It protects essential vagueness, distinguishes an open horizon from a missing explanation, minimises negative constructions, and asks the editor to notice what a cleaner sentence may have lost. Evidence and assumptions remain visible. The prose leaves the reader room to wonder.

## Conviction and the risk of being wrong

Billy added a further distinction: the canon exists to have a point of view, and that view may be wrong. Vulnerability and risk belong to the beauty of the writing. Editorial work should preserve the author's exposure to disagreement.

The guide now gives this principle its own section, “Have a point of view”, and carries it into the revision and handoff checks. It separates accuracy in reporting a source from the author's responsibility for a judgement. Qualifications should inform the reader; they should earn their place as words do elsewhere.

The manuscripts reflect that change. *Symbolic Superintelligence* states its central prediction directly. *Irreducible* again owns its historical philosophical position, with rival accounts available in its appendix. *Computational Abundance* regains its original declaration about the end of scarce intelligence. *Singularity* regains the direct selection-and-love close within its established speculative frame. These are the author's positions, offered for readers to consider and challenge.

## Sources used for targeted corrections

- **Earthrise.** NASA records whole-Earth images before Apollo 8, while identifying Apollo 8 as the first human witnessing of Earthrise from lunar orbit. The revision preserves the human event and drops the broader first. [NASA's history of changing Earth views](https://www.nasa.gov/history/90-years-of-our-changing-views-of-earth/); [the Earthrise account](https://science.nasa.gov/resource/the-story-behind-apollo-8s-famous-earthrise-photo/).
- **Webb.** NASA describes the first deep field as a composite of observations at different wavelengths totalling 12.5 hours, with foreground galaxies magnifying more distant ones. The cited image page does not support attaching the removed 330-million-year age to this image. [The image record](https://science.nasa.gov/asset/webb/webbs-first-deep-field-unveiled-nircam-image/); [the sunshield](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/webb/webbs-sunshield/).
- **M87.** The EHT announcement dates the observations to 2017 and release to 2019, and describes agreement with the expected black-hole shadow. [EHT's announcement](https://eventhorizontelescope.org/press-release-april-10-2019-astronomers-capture-first-image-black-hole).
- **Chess systems.** Stockfish's NNUE documentation places learned evaluation inside search. DeepMind describes AlphaZero as combining learning and search too. The previous “pure-neural” opposition did not hold. [Stockfish's NNUE introduction](https://stockfishchess.org/blog/2020/introducing-nnue-evaluation/); [DeepMind's AlphaZero account](https://deepmind.google/blog/alphazero-shedding-new-light-on-chess-shogi-and-go/).
- **Flux pinning.** CERN's demonstration explains how trapped flux fixes a cooled superconductor relative to a magnet. [The teaching sheet](https://indico.cern.ch/event/1372071/contributions/5799046/attachments/2870239/5024985/Superconductivity%20-%208%20Worksheet%20-%20EN%20-%20laminated.pdf).

Other changes to scope draw on qualifications already present in the essays' own appendices. For example, *Leviathan* Appendix L names land immersion as the fair cooling baseline; *Singularity* Appendix C says a one-kilogram black hole evaporates almost instantly; *The Free Starship* Appendix M says the silicon clock has no honest number until an attrition model exists; *Irreducible* Appendix A explicitly calls its consciousness position a wager.

## Remaining evidential work

The most consequential remaining gap is source precision. *It's All Energy* retains its Zimbabwe scenes without a documented reporting source in its references. Its transmission-cost reference names a class of project appraisal documents rather than a specific document. Those are author/source questions; this review did not invent a provenance for them.

The technical appendices also retain models that deserve separate specialist review. *Bridge to Infinity* combines different cargo and crewed design points and still needs an integrated trajectory and vehicle design. *Leviathan* depends on local ocean conditions and on the distinction between scaling independent workloads and one synchronised run. *Singularity* contains proposed links among reproduction, selection, observer measures, and computation that its equations do not establish empirically. The revisions expose these boundaries more clearly; they do not certify the designs.

Recent financial and AI-performance figures were not independently re-researched as part of this prose pass. A full publication fact-check should resolve precise sources, dates, and denominators, and check that each body claim remains within its source's scope. Existing philosophical and civilisational conclusions remain the author's arguments, not editorially verified facts.
