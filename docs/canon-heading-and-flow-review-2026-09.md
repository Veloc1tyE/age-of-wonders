# The story in the headings

5 September 2026. Editorial review of all eleven canonical essays and both current dossier readers. The author subsequently reviewed the site essays and authorised final targeted fixes and publication. Dossier release remains separate.

## The standard

The author's revisions to *Leviathan*, *It's All Energy*, *Singularity* and *Symbolic Superintelligence* supply the touchstones. A heading sequence should carry events, change scale and return the argument to its human purpose. It should read as a story in miniature. Individual headings can leave room for revelation because the sequence supplies the direction.

“A Supercomputer is Lowered into the Dark” makes an event happen. “Tesseract of Supercomputers” gives scale an image. “A Delivery” leaves the revelation to its paragraph. “We Are Wanderers” brings an economic and epistemic distinction into human experience. “For The Smallest Thing” returns a cosmological ascent to care. These principles, including the removal of redundant announcements of speculation, are formalised in `STYLE_GUIDE.md` under Headings.

## Material coherence corrections in this final window

1. **Aquila's locking comparison.** The stated 158.29 MHz scale is about 19–190 times smaller than 3–30 GHz. The stale 51–510 ratio belongs to the separate 58.79 MHz scale. Only the two live manuscript occurrences were corrected; neither the historical band nor model output was changed. This is a comparison of the stated K scale, not a full-bandwidth ratio.
2. **Computational Abundance H.5: useful operations per dollar.** Costs already defined per useful operation must be inverted directly: `1 / (A + O)`. Multiplying that reciprocal by throughput counted useful work twice. Both allocated costs now explicitly depend on the workload and site.
3. **Computational Abundance H.5: electricity boundary.** The energy floor now uses matched operating IT power, throughput, PUE and actual site electricity cost. A heat-rate ratio changes the fuel component of generation cost; it cannot multiply an entire utility tariff. The illustrative case still gives $0.33 per million tokens for electricity alone.

These are source corrections, not findings in a constructed engine. The Hugging Face interpretation was also corrected: instrumental access-seeking under reduced safeguards does not establish an enduring, task-independent power or survival motive. Peer-goal adoption remains explicit.
